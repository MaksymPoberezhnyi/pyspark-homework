## Change detection with a row hash

To know whether a customer *really* changed, you could compare every tracked column:
`t.name <> s.name OR t.city <> s.city OR …`. That's verbose, breaks on NULLs (`NULL <> 'x'` is
NULL, not true), and you have to edit it every time a column is added. Instead, compute **one hash
over the tracked attributes** (`add_hash`) and compare a single column. Look at its docstring for the
NULL trap it avoids.
